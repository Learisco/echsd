We organize. We uplift. We win.

The Eaton County High School Democrats are a coalition of young leaders working to build a stronger, more inclusive Michigan. We advocate for the issues that matter to students, empower new voices, and create lasting change in our communities.

Join the movement

“Democracy is not a spectator sport. Pull up a chair.”

Who we are

We are high school students from across Eaton County who believe in the power of civic engagement. Our chapter is committed to creating welcoming spaces for discussion, organizing, and action.

From voter registration drives to community service projects, we collaborate with local partners and elected officials to champion progressive values. Whether you're new to politics or already an experienced organizer, there's room for you here.

What we do

  • Host monthly meetings featuring local leaders and activists
  • Organize canvasses, phone banks, and voter registration events
  • Provide leadership development and mentorship opportunities
  • Support candidates who reflect our values and our community
